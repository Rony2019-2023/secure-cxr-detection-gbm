Future Work: Towards a Trustworthy & Robust Medical AIBuilding on this foundational research, our ultimate goal is to transition from standard classification accuracy to deploying Trustworthy, Secure, and Clinically Viable Medical AI. The upcoming roadmap includes:Adversarial Robustness & Cybersecurity for AI: Implementing defense mechanisms (using tools like Adversarial Robustness Toolbox - ART) to secure models against adversarial attacks and malicious manipulations on medical imagery.Federated Learning for Healthcare: Developing privacy-preserving decentralized learning frameworks (using frameworks like Flower) so multiple hospitals can collaboratively train robust diagnostic models without sharing sensitive patient data.Explainable AI (XAI): Integrating visual interpretability techniques such as Grad-CAM and SHAP heatmaps to highlight exact regions in chest X-rays that drive model decisions, fostering trust with clinicians.Rigorous Validation & Cross-Validation: Moving beyond a single split to Stratified k-fold cross-validation, performing patient-level data splitting to prevent data leakage, and conducting multi-center/external validation.Advanced Feature Extraction & Transfer Learning: Incorporating pre-trained deep transfer-learning architectures (ResNet, EfficientNet) and feature dimensionality reduction (PCA) for classical models instead of raw high-dimensional pixels.AuthorsAbisek Dahal, NIT MeghalayaAbu Motaleb Rony, NIT Meghalaya (B.Tech CSE); Instructor, NIST NilphamariEmail: motaleb.rony.phd@gmail.comGitHub: motaleb-roni, Rony2019-2023Soumen Moulik, NIT MeghalayaCitationCode snippet@inproceedings{dahal2024covid,
